# Violence Detection Datasets - Research Summary\n\n## Primary Datasets for SAF-DETR Training\n\n### 1. RWF-2000 Dataset\n**Type**: Real-world CCTV surveillance\n**Size**: 2,000 video clips (1,000 violent, 1,000 non-violent)\n**Duration**: ~5 seconds per clip, ~150 frames average\n**Source**: Real CCTV environments\n**Use Case**: Primary training dataset for violence detection\n**Access**: https://github.com/sayib/RWF2000\n\n**Characteristics**:\n- Real surveillance footage\n- Various lighting conditions\n- Different camera angles\n- Crowded scenes\n- Authentic violence scenarios\n\n### 2. UCF-Crime Dataset\n**Type**: Long-duration surveillance with anomaly segments\n**Size**: 1,900 videos from 13 anomaly classes\n**Duration**: Several minutes per video\n**Source**: Surveillance cameras\n**Use Case**: Anomaly detection, violence localization\n**Access**: https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/projects/real-world/ucf-crime-dataset/\n\n**Characteristics**:\n- Long videos with temporal annotations\n- Multiple anomaly types (including violence)\n- Real-world surveillance scenarios\n- Weakly supervised labels\n\n**Variants**:\n- UCF-Crime (long): Full videos\n- UCF-Crime (short): 1-2 second segments\n\n### 3. Hockey Fight Dataset\n**Type**: Sports violence detection\n**Size**: 1,000 video clips (500 violent, 500 non-violent)\n**Duration**: ~41 frames average (~1.6-2 seconds)\n**Source**: Hockey game footage\n**Use Case**: Baseline violence detection\n**Access**: http://visilab.etsii.uclm.es/personas/oscar/FightDetection.html\n\n**Characteristics**:\n- Controlled environment\n- Clear violence actions\n- Good for initial validation\n- Limited scene diversity\n\n### 4. Movies Fight Dataset\n**Type**: Movie scene violence\n**Size**: 201 videos (100 violent, 101 non-violent)\n**Duration**: ~50 frames average (~1.6-2 seconds)\n**Source**: Movie clips\n**Use Case**: Supplementary training data\n**Access**: Same as Hockey Fight\n\n**Characteristics**:\n- Cinematic quality\n- Various violence types\n- Different lighting conditions\n- Limited realism for surveillance\n\n### 5. ShanghaiTech Campus Dataset\n**Type**: Multi-camera campus surveillance\n**Size**: 13 scenes, 437 videos\n**Duration**: Variable\n**Source**: Campus surveillance\n**Use Case**: Anomaly detection, crowd analysis\n**Access**: https://github.com/StevenLiuWen/surveillance-anomaly-detection\n\n**Characteristics**:\n- Multi-camera setup\n- Campus environment\n- Various anomaly types\n- Good for crowd analysis\n\n### 6. AI Hub CCTV Dataset\n**Type**: Short surveillance clips\n**Size**: Varies\n**Duration**: Short clips\n**Source**: Indoor/outdoor surveillance\n**Use Case**: Rapid action detection\n**Access**: Korean AI Hub platform\n\n**Characteristics**:\n- Short video format\n- Indoor and outdoor scenes\n- Various actions\n- Good for real-time testing\n\n### 7. SCVD Dataset (Surveillance Camera Violence Detection)\n**Type**: CCTV short videos\n**Size**: Varies\n**Duration**: Short sequences\n**Source**: Surveillance cameras\n**Use Case**: Pedestrian and crowd violence\n**Access**: Research paper supplementary\n\n**Characteristics**:\n- Pedestrian-focused\n- Crowd scenarios\n- Anomalous situations\n- Real surveillance quality\n\n### 8. XD-Violence Dataset\n**Type**: Large-scale violence detection\n**Size**: 4,754 videos\n**Duration**: Variable\n**Source**: Multiple sources\n**Use Case**: Multimodal violence detection\n**Access**: https://github.com/Ryuk17/Audiovisual-Violence-Detection\n\n**Characteristics**:\n- Large scale\n- Multimodal (audio + video)\n- Diverse scenarios\n- Weakly supervised\n\n## Dataset Selection Strategy for SAF-DETR\n\n### Primary Training\n1. **RWF-2000**: Main training dataset (real CCTV)\n2. **UCF-Crime**: Secondary training (anomaly diversity)\n3. **Hockey Fight**: Initial validation and testing\n\n### Augmentation Strategy\n- Low-light augmentation\n- Blur augmentation\n- Compression artifacts\n- Noise injection\n- Occlusion simulation\n\n### Evaluation Splits\n- Train: 70% (RWF-2000 + UCF-Crime)\n- Validation: 15% (Mixed datasets)\n- Test: 15% (Hockey Fight + Movies Fight + held-out surveillance)\n\n## Dataset Statistics Summary\n\n| Dataset | Videos | Violent | Non-Violent | Avg Duration | Quality |\n|---------|--------|---------|-------------|--------------|---------|\n| RWF-2000 | 2,000 | 1,000 | 1,000 | ~5s | Real CCTV |\n| UCF-Crime | 1,900 | Varies | Varies | Minutes | Surveillance |\n| Hockey Fight | 1,000 | 500 | 500 | ~2s | Sports |\n| Movies Fight | 201 | 100 | 101 | ~2s | Cinematic |\n| XD-Violence | 4,754 | Varies | Varies | Variable | Mixed |\n\n## Research Findings\n\n### State-of-the-Art Results (2024-2025)\n\n| Model | Dataset | Accuracy | Year |\n|-------|---------|----------|------|\n| MSTFDet | RWF-2000 | 95.20% | 2025 |\n| CUE-Net | RWF-2000 | 94.00% | 2024 |\n| Violence 4D | RWF-2000 | 94.67% | 2023 |\n| IDG-ViolenceNet | RWF-2000 | 94.87% | 2025 |\n| MobileNet | Hockey Fight | 96.66% | 2024 |\n\n### Key Observations\n1. **RWF-2000** is the gold standard for violence detection\n2. **Multimodal approaches** (audio + video) show promise\n3. **Short-window learning** (1-2 seconds) is trending\n4. **Real-time constraints** require lightweight models\n5. **Surveillance-specific** preprocessing is crucial\n\n## Data Preprocessing Requirements\n\n### Video Processing\n- Frame extraction: 15-30 FPS\n- Resolution: 224x224 or 320x320\n- Clip length: 16-32 frames\n- Overlapping windows for temporal coverage\n\n### Augmentation for Surveillance\n- Random brightness/contrast\n- Gaussian blur\n- Compression artifacts\n- Random noise\n- Random occlusion\n- Horizontal flip\n\n### Label Format\n- Binary: Violence / Non-violence\n- Temporal: Frame-level annotations\n- Spatial: Bounding box annotations (if available)\n\n##
